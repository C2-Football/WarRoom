# Your Vault and Duat scouting workspace

## Compare before you decide

Select **Compare** beside up to three draft players. Their cards stay together
above the board. **Quick scout** opens the research pane; selecting a player's
name still opens their full card and game history. Remove a player or clear the
comparison to make room for another.

During the season, use **Compare players** on the Vault roster or the comparison
in Duat's lineup preparation. These views use the game's scoring and the weeks
you have actually revealed. Stars describe the game's stated outlook; read the
short explanation beside them before comparing players. An unavailable value
stays unavailable. Estimated remaining points are marked as estimates.

## Set up your board

Open **View options** to choose comfortable or compact rows and the statistical
columns you want to see. Sorting and display settings are saved in this browser.
Search, comparison and quick-scout selections are kept with the particular
room, seat and army where appropriate. Clearing browser storage clears these
preferences. They do not sync to another device.

## Research while someone else picks

Duat keeps your army's public scouting archive available between turns. You can
search, compare and queue players. Availability is confirmed when your turn
opens; the research archive does not disclose another faction's sealed picks.
Your queue never makes a pick automatically.

## Know whether your move saved

The friends-room strip distinguishes **Synced**, **Saving**, **Offline** and
**Reconnecting**. A confirmed pick highlights your new roster addition. If a move
is not confirmed, keep your selection and reconnect or follow the save-recovery
action before retrying. A rejected sign-in offers account or guest-pass recovery.
Joined and ready labels describe seats; they are not indicators of live presence.

For hosting, guest access and returning on another device, read
[Playing with friends](friends-access.md).
