# Owners, team names, and League life

The Wire identifies the person behind a team without discarding the team name. Current reports carry an owner-and-team byline. Career milestones, archived records, title context, and rivalry profiles lead with the owner; the associated team and season stay visible. Search accepts either name.

Identity comes from an unambiguous documented owner binding within that league, then the verified account's display name or username. A team name is never treated as proof of a person's identity. Missing owner names remain missing, and a reused roster slot cannot transfer history to a replacement manager.

## The lighter side

League life is a separate reading section. One rotating weekly feature appears in a clearly labeled front-page slot; current news retains its own headlines. Features use completed results and actual saved team names:

- Cardiac Club: close games and the people who keep finding them.
- Tough draws: strong scores meeting the wrong opponent.
- Team-name changes: a returning owner, a different name, and the verified seasons.
- Name Game: two real team names offered for debate, with no invented vote or claim that taste is an objective statistic.

Humor is short and about fantasy football circumstances. Facts establish the premise; a light line follows. Missing history never becomes a punchline, a fake quote, or a verdict about someone's ability.

## Draft receipts

The draft desk opens on request and reuses the scored seasons already loaded by The Wire. It fetches draft metadata and picks for the selected season and at most two eligible older seasons. Comparisons concern picks at the same position, verified starting-lineup production, and a clearly stated period. Start counts accompany the totals. They are not ADP grades, injury diagnoses, career rankings, or points credited to the original drafting owner after a trade.

Current-season judgments require enough completed weeks; old seasons require the service's full-coverage checks. Missing observations do not count as zero. Account or edition changes hide old results immediately and cancel pending work. Load failures and timeouts offer a retry.

The multi-league newspaper remains text-only. These features introduce no generated photographs or decorative graphics.
